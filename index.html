Rehenova Technologies respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Password Generator app handles information when you use the app.

The app does not intentionally transmit your password-generation inputs to a developer-operated server. However, information processed by third-party services, such as advertising services, is subject to their own practices. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into the app.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Advertising and audience settings will be configured in accordance with applicable requirements.

You can stop using the app at any time. You can also review your device's advertising privacy settings and any consent options presented to you.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
